About the master class

Cloud-Meds is a two-day, hands-on master class on how artificial intelligence and cloud computing can accelerate drug repurposing for rare genetic diseases. Through talks and practical sessions, participants work with real computational workflows and meet researchers from across the Arab region and Germany.

The master class is organised by the Institut Pasteur de Tunis (IPT) and funded by the Arab-German Young Academy of Sciences and Humanities (AGYA). Participants must bring their own laptop.

Organiser

Institut Pasteur de Tunis (IPT)

A leading Tunisian public health and biomedical research institute, part of the international Pasteur Network.

Funder

Arab-German Young Academy of Sciences and Humanities (AGYA)

An academy of outstanding early-career researchers from the Arab region and Germany, promoting interdisciplinary and international collaboration.

Partner

Helmholtz Institute for Pharmaceutical Research Saarland (HIPS)

A Helmholtz institute in Saarbrücken, Germany, focused on drug discovery and the development of new anti-infective therapies.
